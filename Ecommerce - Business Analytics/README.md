# E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard – Capstone Project (Xaltius)

This project was completed as part of the Xaltius Data Science Certification Internship. It involves building a comprehensive Power BI dashboard to analyze key business metrics for a Shopee Brazil e-commerce dataset. The goal is to extract insights on customers, products, revenue, and shipping to help the company improve decision-making and customer experience.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales performance, customer behavior, and shipping costs
- Identify top-performing products and customer segments
- Support e-commerce business decisions through data storytelling
- Deliver a dynamic Power BI dashboard with interactive filters and visuals

---

## 🗃️ Dataset Overview

- **Source**: Subset of Shopee Brazil data (sanitized for privacy)
- **Time Range**: August 2019 – January 2023
- **Key Features**: Order ID, Customer ID, Product Name, Quantity, Order Date, Shipping Cost, Country, Gender, Age Group, Revenue

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📌 Executive Summary
- **Total Revenue**: ₱351.15M  
- **Total Customers**: 1,000

### 📌 Revenue & Orders Analysis
- Revenue and Order trends over time (YearMonth)
- Revenue breakdown by Country, Gender, and Age Group
- Average Shipping Cost by Country and Order Value

### 📌 Customer Insights
- Revenue contribution by Age Group and Gender
- Top customers by total revenue
- Customer segmentation by country

### 📌 Product Performance
- Top products by quantity and revenue
- Monthly revenue trends per product category
- Interactive product filters and drill-downs

---

## 🔍 Insights

- Revenue is evenly split across male and female customers (~50/50)
- Highest revenue comes from Age Groups 26–45
- Curtains, Wall Art, and Lighting are the top-selling product categories
- Brazil contributes the highest revenue among countries analyzed
- Shipping cost is positively correlated with total purchase value

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-value age groups (26–45)
- Promote best-performing products through featured listings
- Optimize shipping cost by refining logistics for bulk or high-value orders
- Consider country-specific promotions based on revenue contribution
